# Oven trays

Fill a tray, bake it in the oven, then use **Empty the Baking Tray**. An uncooked tray gives the raw food back.

| Tray | Fill with | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| Homemade crisps | potatoes, sweet potatoes, sugar beets, zucchini, kale (mixed) | one portion per vegetable |
| Homemade crisps with oil | the same and oil | more calories and fat, better for the mood |
| Fruit leather | 1 to 8 fruits or berries | one roll per 12 hunger points of fruit; keeps for months |
| Roasted insects | crickets, grasshoppers, sawfly larvae, caterpillars | keep 6 months |

Crisps go into an empty paper bag to stay fresh longer; you get the bag back once they are eaten.

> Capture à faire : tray of crisps in the oven.
