# Drying more food

Everything below uses Food Drying's oven trays, skewers and drying rack, with its usual rules.

| Food | Where |
|---|---|
| Brussels sprouts, cucumber, pumpkin slices, sweet potato, turnip, zucchini, cabbage, sugar beet, garlic, green onions | oven, skewer, drying rack |
| Watermelon slices, rose hips | oven, skewer, drying rack |
| Pineapple, mango, grapefruit: cut them first with **Slice Large Fruit** (knife, 4 slices) | oven, skewer, drying rack (slices only) |
| Jalapeño, habanero | oven, skewer |
| Shrimp, squid, crayfish, oysters: **Salt Seafood** first (5 uses of salt) | drying rack |
| Dandelions, ginger root, grape leaves, violets, thistles, nettles, lemongrass, ginseng | herb drying rack, oven, skewer |

> Capture à faire : drying rack with pineapple slices.

## Seasonings

4 dried basil, chives, cilantro, oregano, parsley, rosemary, sage or thyme and a mortar and pestle give the matching seasoning jar of the game.

## Pemmican

**Make Pemmican** (mortar and pestle): 1 to 4 pieces of dried meat or dried fish fillet and 6 uses of lard or butter. **Make Pemmican with Berries** adds 1 to 4 dried berries. One bar per 20 points of hunger (1 dried beef gives 4 bars); the bars share the nutrition of everything used and keep for a year.
