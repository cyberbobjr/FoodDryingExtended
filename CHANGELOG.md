# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-13

### Added
- Brussels sprouts, cucumber, pumpkin, sweet potato, turnip and zucchini can be dried in the oven, on a skewer and on the drying rack of Food Drying.
- Homemade crisps: a tray of sliced potatoes baked in the oven gives one portion of crisps per potato, which can be put in a paper bag to keep them fresh longer.
