# Changelog

## 1.4.0 — 2026-11-13

### New
- Pemmican: pound 1 to 4 pieces of dried meat with lard or butter (mortar and pestle), with or without dried berries. One bar per 20 points of hunger (1 dried beef gives 4 bars), the bars share the nutrition of everything used and keep for a year. Own look: a pressed brown bar with flecks of fat and berries.

### Fixed
- Rare error when emptying a tray on a dedicated server.

## 1.3.0 — 2026-11-13

### Improved
- Pineapple, mango and grapefruit slices now look like slices: own inventory icons, and the game's watermelon slice shape in the colours of each fruit, in hand and on the ground.
- Dried slices are darker and much thinner than fresh ones.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-13

### Changed
- Large fruits are sliced before drying: the new "Slice Large Fruit" recipe (knife) cuts a pineapple, mango or grapefruit into 4 slices, and the slices are dried. Whole pineapples, mangoes and grapefruits are no longer accepted on trays, skewers and drying racks.
- Pumpkin is dried as pumpkin slices (the game's own slices) instead of a whole pumpkin.
- Fruit leather follows the quantity of fruit: the tray takes 1 to 8 fruits or berries and gives one roll per 12 points of hunger (1 pineapple gives 2 rolls, 4 cherries give 1), with the nutrition shared between the rolls.
- French: fruit leather is now called "Roulé de fruits séchés".

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-13

### Added
- Cabbage, sugar beet, garlic, green onions, watermelon slices and rose hips can be dried in the oven, on a skewer and on the drying rack.
- Jalapeño and habanero peppers can be dried in the oven and on a skewer.
- Salt Seafood: shrimp, squid, crayfish and oysters are salted, then dried on the drying rack like salted fish.
- Crisps from sweet potatoes, sugar beets, zucchini and kale, mixed on the same tray as potatoes.
- Crisps with oil: more calories and fat, and better for the mood.
- Fruit leather: 4 fruits or berries baked into 4 sweet rolls that keep for months.
- Roasted insects: crickets, grasshoppers, sawfly larvae and caterpillars roasted in the oven keep for 6 months.
- Wild plants: dandelions, ginger root, grape leaves, violets, thistles, nettles, lemongrass and ginseng can be dried on the herb drying rack, in the oven or on a skewer.
- Grind into Seasoning: 4 dried basil, chives, cilantro, oregano, parsley, rosemary, sage or thyme and a mortar and pestle give a jar of the matching seasoning.
- German, Spanish, Portuguese, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Turkish translations.

### Changed
- A single "Empty the Baking Tray" recipe empties every tray of this mod; an uncooked tray gives the raw food back.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-13

### Added
- Brussels sprouts, cucumber, pumpkin, sweet potato, turnip and zucchini can be dried in the oven, on a skewer and on the drying rack of Food Drying.
- Homemade crisps: a tray of sliced potatoes baked in the oven gives one portion of crisps per potato, which can be put in a paper bag to keep them fresh longer.
