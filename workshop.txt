description=[h2]More food in Food Drying's recipes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Vegetables: Brussels sprouts, cucumber, pumpkin slices, sweet potato, turnip, zucchini, cabbage, sugar beet, garlic, green onions.
description=[*]Fruit: slices of watermelon, pineapple, mango and grapefruit, rose hips. Jalapeño and habanero peppers in the oven and on a skewer.
description=[*]Wild plants: dandelions, ginger root, grape leaves, violets, thistles, nettles, lemongrass, ginseng.
description=[*]They go into Food Drying's own tray, skewer and drying rack recipes (no extra menu entry). One fresh item gives one dried item with the same nutrition, and it keeps for months.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Notes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Requires Food Drying. Load this mod after Food Drying (and after its VFE patch if you use it).
description=[*]Large fruits are sliced before drying: whole pineapples, mangoes and grapefruits are no longer accepted on trays, skewers and racks. Two files of Food Drying are replaced by updated copies (based on Food Drying 1.11): its drying rack recipes and its fruit tags.
description=[*]Languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Turkish.
description=[/list]
